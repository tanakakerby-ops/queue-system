About Us

What is QueueEase?

QueueEase is an appointment queue system that helps people book a slot, get a queue number, and see when it is their turn.

How it works

  1. The customer fills out the Book page and gets a queue number.
  2. Priority customers (senior, PWD, pregnant) are placed at the front of the line.
  3. The Queue page shows who is being served, and customers can check their own number.
  4. Staff use the Staff page to call the next person.
